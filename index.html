How to Play · 遊

Link touching letters in a straight line
to form a word

붙은 자모를 일자로 이어 단어를 만드세요

Complete a word and its tiles burn to ink — and the word's meaning comes true on the board. Water washes away, fire burns. Clear every tile to finish the stage.

결

Stage Clear · 한 마당 클리어

결

· · ·

 

  • Drag — or tap one by one — to link the letters.
  • 물 (water) flows vertically. Complete it to wash away the noise beside it.
  • 불 (fire) burns horizontally. It scorches the remaining noise.
  • The key: telling real words from the noise.

The Worlds · 界

Twelve worlds,
one hundred forty-four puzzles

열두 개의 세계, 백사십사 개의 마당

The story of creation rests on the frame of the eight trigrams (八卦). Clear a world, and one more scene is painted onto the scroll.

一

意

Meaning is power

These aren't invented symbols — they're real Korean words. Awaken one, and the board obeys its meaning: water soaks, seeds sprout, snow(눈) covers.

二

墨

The feel of ink

Hanji paper, black ink, and a single seal-red. Every solve is a brushstroke — clear a stage, and another landscape is painted on the scroll.

三

☰

A trigram journey

Fire ☲, water ☵, light ☰, stone ☶… the world is created along the eight trigrams — and when you finish, all eight stand complete behind you.
